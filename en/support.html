Forshore: Tide Rescue is a turn-based rescue puzzle for iPhone. Take time to think: the tide advances with your moves, not with a real-time clock.

How rescues work

Move onto neighbouring dry tiles. You automatically pick up people when you have space. Returning to the boat brings them to safety and makes room for the next group. A rope crosses one water tile in a straight line to dry ground.

A 1 on the sand means that tile floods at the end of your next action. Stepping onto it or picking someone up does not stop the tide. Inspecting, changing modes and Undo use no turns.

Open Islands → First Rescues for three lessons. Available actions also lets you choose among the legal destinations for your selected mode.

Free content and the full game

The first six islands, the tutorial and The Nearby Coast are free and replayable. The tutorial and that expedition use the same six islands.

One full-game unlock adds 18 islands and six expeditions. There are no subscriptions or ads. To start Seeded Routes, you must also complete those six expeditions and accept their final results; partial rescues count. Seeds combine existing islands into repeatable routes.

The price available for your account appears on the full-game screen. Opening it does not start a purchase.

I already paid. How do I recover access?

Open Islands → Content and full game → Restore purchases using the Apple Account you purchased with. If the offer does not load, check your connection and use Refresh availability. Restore purchases recovers access to content. Recovering a saved game requires a copy in iCloud; these are separate operations.

If a purchase is awaiting approval, wait for Apple to confirm it. If verification fails, refresh or restore before trying to buy again. If the unlock disappears, Forshore preserves the saved game for paid content and blocks its actions until access returns. You can also choose a free island after confirming the change of game.

Saving, Undo and changing iPhone

Your game and records are saved on this iPhone first, including when you are offline. Undo works even after a failed rescue. Accepting an island’s result in a tutorial or expedition closes that island’s undo history. Changing or restarting asks for confirmation.

You can enable iCloud in Settings. It is optional and free: private copies use your iCloud account, without buying the full game or connecting Game Center. iCloud must be available for Forshore and have enough space. The app syncs when it can; check the status and last sync before changing devices or uninstalling. Pending changes and progress that has not uploaded cannot be recovered on another iPhone.

On a fresh installation, enable iCloud using the same account. If there is one current remote game, it can be recovered when syncing. If another iPhone continues the same game and you have not progressed separately here, that continuation can be applied automatically when the game is ready to update. If the devices have different progress or games without a shared origin, review the copies and confirm which one to continue. The iCloud saving → Review iCloud notice on the game screen opens these options. Available best results and achievements are combined; moves from different games are never mixed. You can also recover an earlier copy from the retained versions.

Turning off iCloud stops syncing and preserves local data and copies already uploaded. Changing iCloud accounts pauses syncing: return to the original account to continue. Progress is not automatically transferred to another account. Game Center is not a saved-game backup; exporting a game for review does not produce a file that can be imported or used to restore progress.

If you have deleted iCloud copies, syncing pauses so they are not uploaded again without your decision. Choose Re-enable iCloud saving in the notice and confirm with the same account. Forshore first checks any remaining copies and can then upload your local game again; it does not delete the game on your iPhone. Cancel or disable the feature if you do not want to save to iCloud again.

If local saving fails, try Retry saving. You can explicitly continue without saving, but those changes may be lost when you close the app and are not uploaded to iCloud until successfully saved. If iCloud fails, you can keep playing locally and sync again after resolving the issue. Avoid uninstalling as the first step when troubleshooting progress.

Language, sound and accessibility

The game includes English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows the app language selected in iOS. Changing language preserves your game.

Settings lets you adjust sound, haptics and guidance. Sound starts switched off. Forshore follows the system’s Reduce Motion setting. With VoiceOver, the actions list opens to offer available destinations without exploring every tile.

Game Center

You can play and earn five local achievements without connecting. Open Settings → Achievements and Game Center to connect, then choose whether to send your saved achievements to the displayed account. Disconnecting from Forshore does not sign out of Game Center on your iPhone.

Report a problem

Email jared.perez.vega@icloud.com with what you expected, what happened, the island, turn, iPhone model and iOS version.

Open Settings → Support information to review the information before sharing. For a rules issue, also export your route before undoing or changing islands. It contains only the current island and its retained moves. You choose whether to send these files and who receives them; the app does not send them automatically.

Do not send passwords or payment details. Read the privacy policy for information about data handling.
