Forshore is a turn-based rescue puzzle for iPhone. Take time to think: the tide advances with your moves, not with a real-time clock.

How rescues work

Move onto neighbouring dry tiles. You automatically pick up people when you have space. Returning to the boat brings them to safety and makes room for the next group. A rope crosses one water tile in a straight line to dry ground.

A 1 on the sand means that tile floods at the end of your next action. Stepping onto it or picking someone up does not stop the tide. Inspecting, changing modes and Undo use no turns.

Open Islands → First Rescues for three lessons. Available actions also lets you choose among the legal destinations for your selected mode.

Free content and the full game

The first six islands, the tutorial and The Nearby Coast are free and replayable. The tutorial and that expedition use the same six islands.

One full-game unlock adds 18 islands and six expeditions. There are no subscriptions or ads. To start Seeded Routes, you must also complete those six expeditions and accept their final results; partial rescues count. Seeds combine existing islands into repeatable routes.

The price available for your account appears on the full-game screen. Opening it does not start a purchase.

I already paid. How do I recover access?

Open Islands → Content and full game → Restore purchases using the Apple Account you purchased with. If the offer does not load, check your connection and use Refresh availability. Restoring access does not recover a deleted saved game.

If a purchase is awaiting approval, wait for Apple to confirm it. If verification fails, refresh or restore before trying to buy again. If the unlock disappears, Forshore preserves the saved game for paid content and blocks its actions until access returns. You can also choose a free island after confirming the change of game.

Saving, Undo and changing iPhone

Your game and records are saved on this iPhone. Undo works even after a failed rescue. Accepting an island’s result in a tutorial or expedition closes that island’s undo history. Changing or restarting asks for confirmation.

Forshore does not sync saved games between devices. Game Center is not a saved-game backup. Exporting a game for review does not create a file that can be imported or used to restore progress.

If saving fails, try Retry saving. You can explicitly continue without saving, but new progress may be lost when you close the app. Avoid uninstalling as the first step when troubleshooting lost progress: there is no Forshore server from which to recover it.

Language, sound and accessibility

The game includes English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows the app language selected in iOS. Changing language preserves your game.

Settings lets you adjust sound, haptics and guidance. Sound starts switched off. Forshore follows the system’s Reduce Motion setting. With VoiceOver, the actions list opens to offer available destinations without exploring every tile.

Game Center

You can play and earn five local achievements without connecting. Open Settings → Achievements and Game Center to connect, then choose whether to send your saved achievements to the displayed account. Disconnecting from Forshore does not sign out of Game Center on your iPhone.

Report a problem

Email jared.perez.vega@icloud.com with what you expected, what happened, the island, turn, iPhone model and iOS version.

Open Settings → Support information to review the information before sharing. For a rules issue, also export your route before undoing or changing islands. It contains only the current island and its retained moves. You choose whether to send these files and who receives them; the app does not send them automatically.

Do not send passwords or payment details. Read the privacy policy for information about data handling.
