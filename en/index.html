Take your time. Make your move.

No timer: the tide only advances when you act. Cross water with your rope, hold the tide back with sandbags, lay planks and choose your mode: Breeze, Tide or Storm. Save everyone on an island to open the next one, in the order of the map. If you get stuck, Help gives you hints.

Start with 14 free islands: the tutorial’s nine lessons, the four Quiet Coves islands and The Breakwater. A one-time unlock adds the other 31 islands of the route. No ads or subscriptions.

Local saves and optional free iCloud sync. Optional Game Center. Play in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese.
